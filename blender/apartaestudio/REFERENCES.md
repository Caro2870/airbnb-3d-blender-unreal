# Apartaestudio de playa: evidence and assumptions

## Sources
- **Floor plan:** the architect's floor plan (1:20), kept privately (not in this repo). Built area 38 m², private area 34.18 m².
- **Photos:** 7 owner photos (Google Drive export, 2026-11-03) in `photos/foto01.jpg` … `foto07.jpg`.
- **Textures, props, HDRI:** Poly Haven (CC0), listed in `assets/sources.json`.
- **Generated furniture:** `assets/gen/*.glb`, made with Hunyuan3D-2.1 (shape only) from photo crops (bed: photo 5;
  balcony set: photo 3; office chair: photo 5). These are generated approximations, not measured models. Tencent

## Assumptions
- Ceiling height 2.50 m; floor level +3.0 m (one storey above grade).
- Furniture and decor follow the photos, not the plan's furniture layout.
- Staging: the sliding leaf is open for the walkthrough, golden-hour sun, haze and dust motes for light beams.
- Surroundings (street, neighbours, sea) are generic stand-ins.
